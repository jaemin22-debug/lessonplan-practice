"Hello, everyone! Nice to see you back today. Did you have a good lunch? Awesome.

Now, do you remember what we talked about with Charlotte's Web last Friday? Yes, we talked about onomatopoeia. We learned that onomatopoeia is a figure of speech mimicking the sounds of animals or inanimate things, such as the wind.

So if we say, 'Pigs make a sound of...?' Yes, 'oink!' How about horses? Yes, they say 'neigh,' mostly. Good job remembering that!

Now, today we will continue to focus on the sounds of English words based on the story Charlotte's Web. This time, we will make a poem out of the story and learn how to make a rhyme. Got it?

What are we going to learn about today? Yes, rhymes! Then let's dive into today's lesson by figuring out what rhyming is first."
